<h5>Nouns in Minecraft</h5>

- "Minecraft" **shouldn't** be translated (e.g. "This is a item in Minecraft" -> "这是一个 Minecraft 里面的物品", which
  keeps "Minecraft")
    - Note: You can translate the "Edition" or "Bedrock Edition" in the subtitle of Minecraft: Java / Bedrock Edition (
      e.g. "Minecraft: Java Edition" -> "Minecraft：Java 版", etc.)
- For other nouns, see
  the [Minecraft Glossary for Crowdin (on Chinese Minecraft Wiki)](https://zh.minecraft.wiki/w/File:Minecraft_Glossary_for_Crowdin.jpg)
    - Or you can go to the ["Minecraft" Project on Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/minecraft)
- For **Non-English** Languages:
    - If the items / entity has no localised translation, you **should** keep its original name instead of translate it.
    - Although you can put the unofficial translation between parentheses.

<h5>Translation Specifications</h5>

- Express meaning is the effect of having language.
    - Literal translation is the main approach, with free translation as a supplement.

- For humourous expressions:
    - Keep its humour in your language.
    - This is an example in Minecraft from English (US) -> English (UK)
        - AmE: ... or eat your pet rock.
        - BrE: ..., or cause your cookie to turn into a "biscuit".

<h5>Typesettings Specifications</h5>

- Try to keep the original format of the text.
- When translating to Chinese, please put a space between Chinese and English or number, but no between English
  or number and Chinese punctuation marks.
    - Also suitable for other languages like Chinese.
